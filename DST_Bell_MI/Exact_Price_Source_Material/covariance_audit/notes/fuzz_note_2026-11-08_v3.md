## 4. Reading (for the casebook; marked)
The census (even harmonics of the approach phases: the sum lock, the slots, the beads) and the rim (odd harmonics: the lean) are two parities of one birth. Trading harmonics in λ₀ against bandwidth in ξ works at a steep rate — each λ₀ harmonic buys back roughly 40% of the remaining smoothness premium — but only when the rim is as finely structured in ξ as the census. So October's grain is not removed by the rim; it is shared between two axes. Whether the premium reaches zero at K = 6 for some L (L = 3 at K = 6 is the next LP; the contraction already holds Lmax = 3) is open; the pre-registered expectation is that it does not.
